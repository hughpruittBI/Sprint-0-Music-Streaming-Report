CONCLUSION


This project analyzed six years of music streaming data across various genres to identify listener trends and potential growth areas. Both free and paid listener counts have steadily increased during this time. However, electronic music consistently ranked as the least played genre, suggesting it may not be worth deeper analysis.
In contrast, indie music outperformed all others in listener engagement, and free listening strategies appear effective in converting users to paid subscribers—evidenced by significant growth in paid listener counts. Rock music is also showing an upward trend and could be a strong candidate for future marketing or content investment.
